# 🎬 Finals Intro — Mia's Script

20 seconds. Mr.-Beast-style hook. Read with energy + dramatic pauses.
The teleprompter at /teleprompter scrolls this at performance pace.

---

## The script

| Beat | Time | Mia says | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–3s  | **"After WEEKS of bracket battles…"** | Slight pause after "weeks" — let it hang |
| 2 | 3–7s  | **"Only FOUR players are still standing."** | Hold up four fingers if she wants |
| 3 | 7–11s | **"Karen versus Marc — winners' bracket final."** | Slow + serious |
| 4 | 11–14s | **"Grandpa versus Sam — losers' last stand."** | Lean forward, intense |
| 5 | 14–17s | **"Fifteen questions. One topic. Two finals."** | Three-beat rhythm, count on fingers |
| 6 | 17–20s | **"The Quiz Book Finals.<br>**Coming soon."*** | "Coming soon" almost whispered |

## Performance notes for Mia

- **EYES**: look right into the camera lens, not the screen
- **VOICE**: louder than normal conversation. Belt it.
- **PAUSES**: the dashes in the script are real pauses. Don't rush them.
- **SMILE**: Beat 1, then go serious. Beat 6 (the "coming soon"), little smirk.
- **HANDS**: keep them mostly out of frame. If she gestures, big and slow.
- **WARDROBE**: bright color (yellow/coral), no busy patterns — they break Remotion's color grade.

## Recording checklist

- [ ] Phone or webcam at eye level (stack books under it if needed)
- [ ] Window light hitting her face (not behind her)
- [ ] Quiet room — no AC humming, no sibling, no background TV
- [ ] Set teleprompter speed to "Medium" first, adjust if too fast/slow
- [ ] Record 3 takes. Pick the best one.
- [ ] Download the .webm file. Drop it at `remotion/public/recording.webm`.
- [ ] Then run `npx remotion render` to get the final cinematic mp4.
